This dashboard tracks how trade policy is shaping the global green transition. Its purpose is diagnostic: to assess the extent to which governments' trade and industrial policy choices support or undermine the shift away from fossil fuels. All indicators and visualisations are derived directly from GTA intervention records and CLAIRK's coding of FTA environmental provisions. Data is refreshed daily, except for FTA-related information.

Methodology

About the data

The indicators in this dashboard are built using two types of data. Dynamic indicators are derived directly from the Global Trade Alert database and refreshed daily via the GTA API. Static indicators are based on CLAIRK's database coding of environmental provisions in free trade agreements. This data is only updated periodically.

All dynamic indicators include measures covering the product groups specified in the "product groups" section. All product groups use the HS Nomenclature 2022 version at 6 digits. The trade instruments covered have been grouped into different policy categories as specified in the section "policy groups". Only measures with an announcement date from 1 January 2020 are included.

The GTA evaluates each intervention as Red (almost certainly harmful to foreign commercial interests), Amber (likely harmful), or Green (almost certainly liberalising). In this dashboard, "harmful" refers to Red and Amber interventions combined; "liberalising" refers to Green interventions. The underlying dataset and all indicators can be downloaded using the buttons in the banner above.

Green Transition Policy Behaviour

Net Policy Orientation on Green Goods

Count of liberalising interventions minus harmful interventions on green goods HS codes, per implementing jurisdiction. Positive = net liberalising; negative = net harmful.

Top Implementers — Green Sector Support

Combines: (a) harmful subsidies (MAST L) on green goods, hydrogen, and strategic minerals; (b) liberalising import barriers on the same products; (c) liberalising export controls on the same products. Cumulative since January 2020.

Growth of Green Industrial Policy

Annual counts of all intervention types (harmful and liberalising) affecting green goods, hydrogen, and strategic minerals HS codes, announced since 2020.

Fossil Fuel Persistence

Active Fossil Fuel Subsidies

Cumulative count of in-force subsidy interventions (MAST Chapter L) on coal, oil, gas, and petrochemicals HS codes per implementing jurisdiction, restricted to measures currently in force.

Fossil Fuel vs Green Subsidy Trends

Annual counts of subsidy interventions (MAST Chapter L) on fossil fuel HS codes versus green goods, hydrogen, and strategic mineral HS codes.

Green Protectionism

Leading Users of Trade Remedies on Green Goods

Cumulative counts of anti-dumping, anti-subsidy, safeguard, and anti-circumvention interventions where at least one affected product falls within the green goods HS code list.

Export Restrictions on Energy Transition Minerals

Annual counts of export control interventions on HS codes for lithium, copper, cobalt, nickel, graphite, and rare earths separately.
